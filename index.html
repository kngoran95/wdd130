Grand-Bassam

Grand-Bassam is a very popular tourist city in Côte d’Ivoire. It was the country's first colonial capital from 1893 to 1900 and has a rich historical and cultural heritage. With its well-preserved colonial architecture and beautiful beaches, the city is now a UNESCO World Heritage Site and attracts many visitors from around the world every year.
